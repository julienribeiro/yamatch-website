## Revision v2 — more natural club reportage (2026-10-14)

Seven independent edits using the built-in `image_gen` tool with each original PNG as its sole `referenced_image_paths` edit target. All seven originals were visually inspected before editing; the original PNG and WebP files remain untouched. The selected `*-v2.png` outputs remain AI-generated illustrative scenes, not photographs of an actual event. Delivery copies `*-v2.webp` use `cwebp -q 85`, encoding only, without crop, resize, pixel retouching or compositing. Every v2 asset remains 1086 × 1448 (3:4).

The revision reduces the coordinated teal/coral palette, golden-hour lighting and polished model-like expressions, using neutral outdoor light, normal municipal gyms, practical clothing and more spontaneous expressions. All seven outputs were visually inspected for story, framing, hands and equipment. Some background detail and small equipment marks remain synthetic; realism is a visual direction, not a claim of documentary provenance.
